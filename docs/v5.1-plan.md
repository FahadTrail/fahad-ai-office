# V5.1 plan: simple outside, powerful inside

This plan covers the branch `claude/v5-immersive-office` only. It must **NOT
be merged** until V4.1 reports **V4 CLOSED** *and* Fahad approves the
immersive direction.

## 1. Information architecture

The principle is **simple outside, powerful inside**. The first level shows
five things. Everything else moves one level deeper and stays reachable.
Nothing is deleted.

| Level 1 (sidebar) | What it answers | Level 2 (inside) | Level 3 (deep detail) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chief** (chat) | "What do I want done?" | Recent chats, All chats | Conversation, task timeline |
| **Employees** | "Who is doing what?" | Employee page: Now / Work / Chat | Artifacts, handoffs, history, tools |
| **Projects** | "Where does each project stand?" | Command Center: summary, then the map | Workflow, tasks, memory, settings |
| **Needs Fahad** | "What is waiting for me?" | Grouped by urgency | The approval or question itself |
| **Office** | "Show me the team working" | Immersive / Light Office | Employee focus, handoff, artifact |
| *More* (collapsed) | — | Tasks, Artifacts, Models, Integrations, Settings | Existing pages |

**Rules for every screen:**
1. **Open with one human summary sentence.** For example: "3 people are
   working; FINANCE finished the budget; 1 thing needs you." Tables come after
   the summary, never first.
2. **At most one primary action** per screen.
3. **Technical words go deeper.** "Route", "provider", "tokens", "cooldown"
   appear only on Models (under More) and in deep detail.
4. **Labels use the employee's name and a plain verb:** "FINANCE is checking
   the numbers", not "SPECIALIST_WORKING".
5. **Progressive disclosure:** summary → a short list (at most 5 rows, then
   "Show all") → the full table.

**Status:**

    "All chats"), Employees, Projects, Needs Fahad, Office.
  * The other items sit under a collapsed **More**, which opens automatically

  * **Done:** one summary sentence on Employees, Needs Fahad, Tasks,

## 2. 3D visual upgrade

The target is a premium, architectural, calm Office. It must not look like a
game, must not be neon, and must not use childish characters.

| Area | Now | V5.1 target | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lighting | Hemisphere, sun and fill lights | **Done:** image-based lighting (RoomEnvironment through PMREM) on high/balanced, so wood, metal, glass and screens reflect. Day exposure is kept close to V5; night is brighter and more legible. | Next: time-of-day warmth (light temperature follows the local hour) |
| Materials | Flat standard materials | **Done:** tuned roughness/metalness (brushed metal, satin wood, glossier bezels, clearer glass). Next: subtle normal detail on the floor and wood (a small generated canvas noise; no downloads). | `MeshStandardMaterial` plus canvas textures |
| Floor | Plank canvas texture | Large-format stone in the atrium, oak in the wings, soft rugs per wing | Canvas textures per zone |
| Glass | Transparent planes | Frosted glass with a thin metal frame and a soft edge highlight | Frame geometry plus env reflections |
| Architecture density | Sparse plinth | Ceiling baffles or light slots (render-only, no shadows), columns at the wing boundaries, a reception desk at the entrance, bookshelves in Intelligence | Merged static geometry (keep under 200 draw calls) |
| Furniture | Simple desks | Rounded desk tops with an edge chamfer, task lamps (warm emissive at night), cable trays, monitor arms | Reuse the merge pipeline |
| Characters | Capsule figures | Proportioned mannequins: a separate shoulder volume, a slight torso taper, hands, and a hair or head-shape variant per role. Calm idle motion. No faces. | `characters.js` only (a replaceable module) |
| Workspace identity | Accent collar | A wing material accent (rug, chair fabric, board frame) plus the role's real artifact on its wall | Existing wing accents |
| Camera | Tween between presets | Cinematic ease with a slight arc (dolly plus crane), depth-of-field only on high | Camera rig; the DOF pass is optional behind the quality tier |
| Night | Darker palette | Warm desk lamps and softly lit screens; the atrium dimmed; the window wall shows dusk | Emissive materials; no bloom (keeps the cost low) |

**Budgets (keep them enforced by tests):**
* engine at most 170 KB gzipped (now about 150 KB);
* scene code at most 40 KB gzipped for all `office3d/` modules (now about
  34 KB; the old 90 KB raw limit was replaced when lighting and decor moved
  into their own modules);
* at most about 200 overview draw calls (now 166);
* the light tier keeps no IBL and no shadows.

## 2b. Done in the V5.1 visual polish pass (2026-09-28)

* **Character V2** (`characters.js`): people instead of mannequins, with a
  department wardrobe; 7 draw calls per figure instead of 12.
* **Time of day** (`lighting.js`): DAY, EVENING and NIGHT presets for the
  sun, sky, fill, reflections, exposure, lamps, pools of light, screen glow,
  wall washes, department accents, screen brightness and the stage backdrop.
  They follow the theme, or Fahad picks one under Office → View → Light. The
  change blends over 2.2 s (instant with reduced motion). No clock, location
  or weather is read.
* **Light without lights** (`scene.js`, `decor.js`): additive decals for
  pools of light, screen glow, daylight patches and wall washes; emissive
  pendants, task lamps and a floor light cove. No extra real lights.
* **Architecture** (`decor.js`): glazed back and left facades, suspended
  linear pendants over every desk, a stone inlay under CHIEF, archive
  cabinets and a reading table (Intelligence), a walnut boardroom table, an
  executive credenza and a framed print (Strategy), a standing studio table,
  a felt sofa and warm acoustic baffles (Creative), a rack pair, a workbench
  and dark baffles (Build), and department accent strips under every wall
  display.
* **Cutaway:** suspended fixtures show in the overview only, so nothing
  hangs between the camera and the work in close views.
* **Camera:** department views (Office → Area), framing that keeps the
  employee, the desk and the whole wall display together, smootherstep
  easing, and a slight crane on long moves.
* **Performance:** ambient-only motion renders at 30 fps; a still Office
  does not render.

**Measurement:**
* `node tools/office3d-shots.mjs <dir>` and `tools/v5-preview-build.mjs`,
  then the screenshots A–P (see `docs/v5-immersive-office.md`).
* Real-GPU FPS is still unmeasured. SwiftShader FPS dropped from about 5 to
  about 2 with IBL on the high tier, which is expected for software
  rendering. Fahad's desktop panel shows the real FPS.
